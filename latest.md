# News Digest — 15 November 2026

## Top 10

1. Bitcoin's slide got worse. It fell below 63 thousand dollars, its lowest in three months, and one analyst calls it a fourth wave of capitulation, meaning people are now selling at prices they wished they'd bought at three months ago. (CoinDesk)
2. Iran's navy says three tankers are on fire after hitting mines in the Strait of Hormuz. Earlier, a Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted missiles fired toward Riyadh and drones over the Eastern Province. (Reuters)
3. Interest rate hikes are now the main conversation at central banks. Boston Fed president Susan Collins says gradual rate hikes are the best way to manage the risks, and Bank of England official Huw Pill told the FT that the bank will likely raise rates further next year. (Reuters)
4. Lithuania's prime minister, Mindaugas Sinkevičius, says there will be no changes to income-related taxes next year, and no changes to pension indexation while he leads the government. That came after criticism from within his own party of ideas to slow pension increases. (LRT, 15min, Delfi)
5. Trump and Putin are pushing for a three-way meeting with Xi Jinping in China, which some are calling a new Yalta, but Beijing seems wary. Separately, the US and China discussed tariffs and farm trade ahead of a Xi–Trump meeting. (Reuters, LRT, 15min)
6. President Zelensky says the US floated the idea of Ukraine giving up the Donbas region in exchange for NATO membership. He also called Putin's demands on European security dangerous. (Reuters, 15min, LRT)
7. Lithuania's foreign ministry met Chinese officials, and the prime minister says that lays the groundwork for restoring diplomatic representation between the two countries. (LRT, 15min)
8. US stocks mostly eased on Friday, dragged down by tech and worries around Nvidia. Elon Musk weighed in, saying Nvidia will lose critical deals without Jensen Huang. (Reuters)
9. The US air travel chaos isn't over. The FAA says disruptions will last until late Sunday, the outage is being blamed on a router installed by L3Harris, and more flights are being cancelled. (Reuters, LRT, 15min)
10. Italy fined Meta 887 million dollars over how it accessed data from Android users. (Reuters)
