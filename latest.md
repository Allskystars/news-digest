# News Digest — Monday, 9 November 2026

## Top 10

1. Nvidia says it is investigating its own chief executive, Jensen Huang, after he admitted to an affair with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy. The company is also checking whether he was a target of foreign spying. President Trump called Huang "honorable." (BBC, Reuters)
2. Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies slid to two-month lows after the US midterm elections, then bounced a little. The Nvidia scandal and stalled Iran talks added to the swings. (CoinDesk, Reuters)
3. Trump says he is unlikely to order new attacks on Iran if talks make progress. Oil prices fell on the news, though Tehran says talks have not moved since the midterms. (Reuters, Delfi)
4. Democrats won big in the US midterms, and now plan investigations into figures like Elon Musk and Stephen Miller. Some Trump supporters are asking whether the movement has hit a wall. (Reuters, LRT)
5. A Houthi drone and missile attack damaged the Aramco tower in the financial district of Riyadh. (BBC, Reuters)
6. Poland has declared national mourning after a mass shooting in Krakow. Police say the attacker was highly skilled with weapons, and his motives are still unclear. (Reuters, Delfi, 15min)
7. Lithuanian business groups are angry about a new rule on extra days off for grandparents. Separately, the government's plan to tax Orlen's extra profits is being called a mistake by economists. (LRT)
8. Russia says it hit power substations in Ukraine and three ships in the Black Sea. Power was restored at the Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant. (Reuters)
9. China extended its pause on a 24 percent tariff on US imports by two more months. (Reuters)
10. Anthropic has reportedly secured a 3.4 billion dollar deal for power generators to run its AI data centers. (Reuters, via the Financial Times)
