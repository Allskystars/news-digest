# News Digest — Friday, 6 November 2026

## Top 10

2. South Korean stocks fell about eight percent and triggered a circuit breaker as chip shares tumbled. Analysts see it as part of a wider rout in the AI trade. (Reuters)
3. The Bank of England raised interest rates for the first time in three years and warned that more hikes are likely. Gilt yields rose. (Reuters, BBC)
4. Bitcoin slid to around seventy-six thousand dollars as the collapsing AI trade dragged stocks lower. Stocks had just closed their worst day since April 2025. (CoinDesk)
5. Oil is heading for a sharp weekly drop as signs of a supply glut appear. Tanker attacks near the Strait of Hormuz are keeping a floor under prices. (Reuters)
6. Lithuania says it is ready to host more US troops. The prime minister said about fifty American soldiers will move from Poland to Pabradė. (LRT, 15min)
7. Lithuania expelled three Ukrainians suspected of working for foreign intelligence. The security service says they were stopped before doing any real damage. (LRT)

9. President Nausėda called for a referendum on the definition of family, backed by a change to the Constitution. (LRT)
10. A $6.3 billion hack at Binance is still sending shockwaves through crypto. Rival exchanges are being asked to prove they could survive something similar. (CoinDesk)
