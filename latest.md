# News Digest — Friday, 16 October 2026

## Top 10

1. Global markets are in the middle of an AI-driven selloff. Reuters says US chip stocks are set to lose around two trillion dollars in value, and the pain is spreading to credit markets.
2. CoreWeave is at the center of the wipeout. Reuters reports the AI cloud company's slump is shining a light on the heavy debt behind the AI boom.
3. Insurance against AI-related credit defaults has hit a record high, according to Reuters, and investors are asking whether bonds are becoming a safe place again.
4. South Korea's Kospi closed at a six-month low as chipmakers slumped on AI worries, and CoinDesk says it dropped as much as eight percent at one point. Reuters also reports MSCI is getting ready to remove Indonesia from its global indexes, sending that market to a six-year low.
5. Bitcoin fell below eighty thousand dollars for the first time in a month, then bounced back to around eighty-two thousand. CoinDesk says nearly a billion dollars of leveraged crypto bets were wiped out.
6. Lithuania has frozen the assets of Russian oligarchs, including Alisher Usmanov and Mikhail Fridman, even though they were taken off another sanctions list. The Wall Street Journal and others covered it.
7. Lithuania and Ukraine are teaming up on drones. Ukrainian electronic-warfare systems called Damba are to be built in Lithuania, with a plant planned for 2027, LRT reports.
8. Kraken's parent company, Payward, is buying Lithuania's Saldo Bank, which makes it the first crypto company to acquire a bank in the EU.
9. Lithuania's government is looking at next year's state, Sodra and health insurance budgets. LRT says the Sodra budget is expected to run a surplus of about one point four four billion euros.
10. In Britain, the economy grew by zero point one percent in August, surprising analysts who expected a small contraction. The BBC says the chancellor calls it a sign of resilience ahead of the Budget.
