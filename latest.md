# News Digest — 11 November 2026

## Top 10

1. US inflation came in hotter than expected. Consumer prices rose 0.4 percent in October, and a Fed rate hike in December now looks all but certain, with Governor Waller even floating a half-point move. (Reuters, CoinDesk)

9. Oil rose from a two-month low as attacks on shipping near the Strait of Hormuz offset hopes for a truce with Iran. (Reuters)
10. Taiwan says it will not rename its representative office in Vilnius, and the Lithuanian presidency has responded. The parliament's foreign affairs committee will look into the situation, while a group of MPs is set to host a Chinese delegation. (Focus Taiwan, 15min, LRT)
