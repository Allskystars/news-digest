# News Digest — Saturday, 7 November 2026

## Top 10

1. US jobs report came in far stronger than expected: 165,000 jobs added in October against a forecast of 65,000, and unemployment fell to 4.1 percent. (Reuters, CoinDesk)
2. After that report, Reuters says some Fed policymakers are now considering a rate hike. (Reuters)
3. The AI-led tech selloff kept rattling markets. Trillions were wiped off share values, the Nasdaq posted a fourth straight weekly fall, and the FTSE 100 ended the week lower. (BBC, Reuters)
4. The selloff was partly triggered by fears that the Democratic midterm win means tougher scrutiny of AI and data centres. (BBC)
5. In Lithuania, more than 6,000 NATO troops, led by Germany, are starting large exercises, testing allied combat readiness near the Belarus border. (LRT, Anadolu)

7. Lithuania's National Audit Office warned the budget deficit could approach six percent of GDP by 2029. Ministers will need more than a defence-spending argument to explain it. (LRT)
8. Bitcoin hovered just under 76,000 dollars after the tech rout. More than a billion dollars of bullish bets were wiped out, and ETFs shed about 430 million dollars. (CoinDesk)
9. Tesla is set to end the Cybertruck after poor sales, to make room for its driverless Cybercab. (Reuters, BBC)
10. A court found the Lithuanian company Grigeo Klaipėda guilty in the Curonian Lagoon pollution case and ordered large compensation. (LRT, 15min)
